# Creating Your Account

Getting started with Nupat Cloud takes less than 2 minutes. Follow the steps below to register your account.

## Step 1: Visit the Registration Page

Navigate to [nupatcloud.com/register](https://nupatcloud.com/register).

## Step 2: Provide Your Details

Enter your details:
- **First Name & Last Name**
- **Work Email Address** (This will be your primary login and billing notification channel)
- **Password** (Must be at least 8 characters with numbers and symbols)
- **Phone Number & Company Name**
- **Billing Address, City, State, Country, and Postcode**

## Step 3: Complete Sign Up

Click **Create Account**. A 6-digit one-time verification code (OTP) will immediately be sent to your email address.
