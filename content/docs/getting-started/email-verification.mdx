# Email Verification

To protect your account and ensure legitimate ownership, Nupat Cloud requires email verification before provisioning cloud infrastructure.

## Entering the 6-Digit Code

1. Check your email inbox for a message from `no-reply@nupatcloud.com`.
2. Copy the 6-digit OTP code.
3. Paste the code into the verification form on [nupatcloud.com/verify](https://nupatcloud.com/verify).
4. Click **Verify Account**.

## Didn't receive the email?

- Check your **Spam** or **Promotions** folder.
- Click **Resend Code** on the verification screen.
- Wait at least 60 seconds between resend requests.
