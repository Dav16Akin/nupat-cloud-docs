# Payment Methods & Currencies

Nupat Cloud supports local payment methods in Nigerian Naira (NGN) and international credit cards.

## Supported Channels via Paystack

- **Debit / Credit Cards**: Mastercard, Visa, Verve.
- **Direct Bank Transfer**: Instant virtual account generation.
- **USSD**: GTBank, Zenith, Access, UBA, and all major commercial banks.
- **Apple Pay**: Instant checkout on iOS and macOS devices.
