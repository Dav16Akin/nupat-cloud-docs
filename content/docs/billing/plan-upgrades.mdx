# Upgrading Your Hosting Plan

1. Log in to the [Dashboard](https://nupatcloud.com/dashboard/hosting).
2. Select your hosting account.
3. Click **Upgrade Plan**.
4. Choose your new tier (e.g. Starter to Business, or Business to Agency).
5. Pay the prorated invoice amount.
6. The increased storage and CPU quotas are applied instantly.
