# Invoices & Receipts

1. Sign in to your [Dashboard](https://nupatcloud.com/dashboard/invoices).
2. Click **Invoices** in the sidebar.
3. Locate any paid or pending invoice.
4. Click **Download PDF** or **View Invoice** to get official tax receipts for accounting.
