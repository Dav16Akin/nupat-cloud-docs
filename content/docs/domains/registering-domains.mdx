# Registering a Domain

Register `.com`, `.ng`, `.com.ng`, `.africa`, `.io`, and 50+ extensions with instant activation.

## Registration Process

1. Go to [nupatcloud.com/domains](https://nupatcloud.com/domains).
2. Enter your desired domain name in the search bar.
3. Review availability, 1st-year pricing, and renewal rates across TLDs.
4. Click **Add to Cart**.
5. (Optional) Check the **Bundle SSL** option if you require dedicated certificate provisioning.
6. Click **Checkout** and complete payment via Paystack.
7. Your domain is registered instantly in the global registry.
