# Transferring a Domain to Nupat Cloud

Transferring your domain brings all your services under a single dashboard with zero website downtime.

## Prerequisites for Transfer

- The domain must be at least **60 days old**.
- Domain status must be **Unlocked** at your current registrar.
- You must obtain the **EPP / Auth Code** from your current registrar.
- Privacy protection (WHOIS Privacy) should be temporarily disabled.

## Step-by-Step Transfer

1. Go to your current registrar (e.g. Namecheap, GoDaddy, Whogohost) and unlock the domain.
2. Request your **Auth / EPP Code**.
3. Visit [nupatcloud.com/dashboard/domain-transfer](https://nupatcloud.com/dashboard/domain-transfer).
4. Enter your domain name and Auth Code.
5. Add to Cart and complete payment.
6. The registry will process the transfer within 5–7 days.
