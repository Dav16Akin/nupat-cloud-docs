# Nameservers & DNS Management

## Default Nupat Cloud Nameservers

To connect your domain to Nupat Cloud hosting, point your nameservers to:
```
ns1.nupatcloud.com
ns2.nupatcloud.com
```

## Changing Nameservers in Dashboard

1. Sign in to your **Dashboard** > **Domains**.
2. Click on your active domain.
3. Scroll to **Nameserver Settings**.
4. Select **Use Default Nameservers** or enter custom nameservers.
5. Click **Save Nameservers**. (Propagation takes between 1 and 24 hours).

## Managing DNS Records in cPanel

1. In cPanel, navigate to **Zone Editor**.
2. Click **Manage** beside your domain.
3. Add or edit `A`, `CNAME`, `MX`, `TXT`, and `SRV` records.
