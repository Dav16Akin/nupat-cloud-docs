# Automated Free SSL Certificates

Every domain and subdomain pointed to Nupat Cloud servers automatically receives an **AutoSSL** certificate (Let's Encrypt) with 256-bit encryption.

## How AutoSSL Works

- As soon as your domain's DNS points to your server IP, the AutoSSL background service verifies the domain.
- A valid TLS 1.3 certificate is issued within 30 minutes.
- AutoSSL renews automatically every 90 days with zero manual intervention required.
