# Accessing Your cPanel Control Panel

Nupat Cloud provides two convenient ways to access your cPanel control panel.

## Method 1: One-Click Login from Dashboard (Recommended)

1. Sign in to your [Nupat Cloud Dashboard](https://nupatcloud.com/dashboard).
2. Click **Hosting** in the sidebar.
3. Select your active hosting account.
4. Click the **Launch cPanel** button.
5. You will be logged in automatically without entering credentials.

## Method 2: Direct cPanel URL

You can access cPanel directly in your browser:
- `https://yourdomain.com:2083` or `https://cpanel.yourdomain.com`
- Enter your **cPanel Username** and **Password** (provided in your welcome email and hosting dashboard).
