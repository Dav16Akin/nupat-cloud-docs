# Setting Up Business Email Accounts

Create customized corporate email addresses (e.g. `info@yourcompany.com`) directly from cPanel.

## Creating an Email Address

1. In **cPanel**, navigate to **Email** > **Email Accounts**.
2. Click **+ Create**.
3. Select your domain name from the dropdown.
4. Enter your desired username (e.g. `contact`, `sales`, `hello`).
5. Enter a secure password.
6. Allocate storage quota (or select Unlimited).
7. Click **Create**.

## Accessing Webmail

- Visit `https://yourdomain.com/webmail` or `https://yourdomain.com:2096`.
- Sign in with your full email address and password.
- Choose **Roundcube** to read and compose emails.
